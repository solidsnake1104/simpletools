  COLLECTING ──┬─ has egg?
               ├─ has flour?

      │ (all 3 collected)
      ▼
  ALL_ITEMS
      │ (talk to Cook)
      ▼
  TURNING_IN
      │ (items removed, rewards given)
      ▼
  COMPLETE
