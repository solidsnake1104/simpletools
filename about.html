About SimplerTools

A small collection of tools designed to be quick, useful, and respectful of your privacy.

SimplerTools is a collection of fast, free, privacy-focused browser tools designed to make everyday tasks easier. All tools run entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded, stored, or tracked.

Our Mission

We believe tools should be simple, private, and accessible to everyone. No clutter. No bloat. No unnecessary data collection.

What We Offer

  • Browser-based utilities for everyday tasks
  • Fast, lightweight tools that work locally in your browser
  • A straightforward experience with no account required

More tools are added regularly as we continue expanding the SimplerTools platform.
